## Aesthetic
Dark "conductor's studio at night." Amber-gold is the hero (the Maestro's baton/podium light);
violet is the secondary (AI/edge). Deep near-black canvas, thin cool-gray hairlines, generous
negative space, one confident accent per surface. Premium, calm, not neon-slop.

## Type
Clean geometric/grotesque sans for headings + humanist sans for body (system stack:
Inter / SF / Helvetica Neue family). Tight leading on headings, airy on body.

## Voice
Decisive, builder-first, plain-language. "It acts, you approve." The axiom: "Ship it or it didn't exist."
Tasteful emoji in Discord only; never on the website (NO-EMOJI website law).

## Logo concept
A conductor's baton stroke that doubles as a rising "ship" vector / upward tick — amber on
near-black, with the wordmark "VibeMaestro" (studio) or "VibeBuilders" (community).
